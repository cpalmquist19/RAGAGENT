## Policy Statement


This Policy establishes the various classifications of and qualifications for a teacher license.

## Policy

1. A teacher license shall entitle the licensee to teach in license area(s) indicated on the license.
2. Except as provided in Section 5 below, to qualify for a teacher license, an applicant must have completed an educator preparation program at one of the following levels:
  (a) Bachelor’s Degree (A – Level)
  (b) Master’s Degree (M – Level)
  (c) Specialist Degree (S – Level)
  (d) Doctoral Degree (D – Level)
3. An applicant is only eligible for a license at the highest degree level at which the applicant completed an EPP; however, the applicant may qualify for graduate salary pay under LICN-006.
4. An applicant with a high school diploma (or equivalent) or associate’s degree (V-Level) may be issued a teaching license in the following license areas, provided the applicant meets all other requirements for the license classification sought by the application, as provided in Section 5.
  (a) Career and Technical Education
  (b) Junior Reserve Officer Training Corps
  (c) Safety and Driver Education
5. Each teacher license shall be classified as one of the following based on the applicant’s qualifications, in accordance with the corresponding rule:
  (a) Initial Professional License, as provided in LICN-020.
  (b) Continuing Professional License, as provided in LICN-024.
  (c) Residency License, as provided in LICN-019.
  (d) Limited License, as provided in LICN-023.
  (e) Permit to Teach, as provided in LICN-017.
  (f) Emergency License, as provided in LICN-018.
  (g) International Faculty License, as provided in LICN-026.
  (h) Lifetime License, as provided in LICN-027.
  (i) Provisional License, as provided in LICN-014.
