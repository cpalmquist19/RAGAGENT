## Policy Statement


The LICN Policy Series sets forth requirements related to the licensing of professional educators in North Carolina. It is the policy objective of the State Board of Education to ensure that all North Carolina public schools are served by highly qualified, teachers, administrators, and student services personnel dedicated to the mission of the State Board of Education and the Department of Public Instruction to provide every student an excellent education that prepares them for success in their next phase of life.

## Policy

1. **Delegation of Authority** In accordance with GOVR-005 and except as otherwise provided in this policy series, the State Board of Education (“SBE”) hereby delegates to the Superintendent of Public Instruction (“Superintendent”) the authority to comply with, execute, and administer all applicable state and federal laws and the needed rules and regulations of the SBE related to professional educator licensure. Except as otherwise provided by this policy series, the Superintendent may further delegate such authority to the staff and officers of the Department of Public Instruction (“DPI”).
2. **Definitions** As used in this Policy Series, the following definitions apply:
  (a) “Administrator” means an administrator or supervisor who serves in general and program administrative roles, as classified by the SBE. Administrators shall include superintendents, assistant or associate superintendents, principals, assistant principals, and curriculum-instructional specialists.
  (b) “Administrator license” means a five-year renewable license issued to an individual who meets the requirements of LICN-011.
  (c) “Administrator preparation program” or “APP” means a program approved by the State Board of Education to prepare, train, and recommend candidates for administrator licensure, and that means the requirements of G.S. 115C-284.1 and TCED-015.
  (d) “Applicant” means a person applying for any of the licensure services listed in LICN-008.
  (e) “Beginning teacher” means a professional educator who holds a license other than a Continuing Professional License or a Limited License.
  (f) “Clear license” means a professional educator license or equivalent that:
    (i) Is in good standing with the licensing authority that issued the license;
    (ii) Does not require the completion of any additional professional examinations or coursework;
    (iii) Has not been revoked or had discipline imposed by any jurisdiction;
    (iv) Does not have an investigation relating to unprofessional conduct pending in any jurisdiction relating to the license; and
    (v) Has not been voluntarily surrendered while under investigation for unprofessional conduct in any jurisdiction.
  (g) “Continuing education unit” or “CEU” means credit earned by a professional educator for the purpose of renewing the educator’s license. One CEU shall consist of either:
    (i) Two-thirds of a semester credit hour from an institution of higher education; or
    (ii) Ten clock hours of professional development programming.
  (h) “Continuing professional license” or “CPL” means a five-year, renewable license issued to an applicant or professional educator who meets the relevant requirements established by SBE policy.
  (i) “Emergency license” or “EL” means a one-year nonrenewable license issued to an individual who meets the requirements of LICN-018.
  (j) “EVAAS” means the Education Value-Added Assessment System.
  (k) “Initial professional license” or “IPL” means a three-year, nonrenewable license issued to an applicant or professional educator who meets the relevant requirements established by SBE policy.
  (l) "Jurisdiction" means any of the following:
    (i) A state, territory, or federal district of the United States.
    (ii) A foreign country or political subdivision thereof.
  (m) “License” means a professional educator license issued by DPI in accordance with SBE policy and Chapter 115C, Article 17E of the General Statutes.
  (n) “License area” means a grade level, content area, or specialization in which a license authorizes the licensee to practice.
  (o) “Licensure examination requirements” means the standardized examination requirements adopted by the State Board of Education pursuant to G.S. 115C-270.15 for each license classification. Licensure examination requirements are set forth in LICN-003.
  (p) “Lifetime license” means a license requiring no renewal issued to an individual who meets the requirements of LICN-027.
  (q) “Limited license” means a three-year renewable license issued to an individual who has completed an EPP, but has not satisfied the applicable licensure examination requirements, and means the requirements of LICN-023.
  (r) “Out-of-state applicant” means any applicant who completed an educator preparation program or administrator preparation program with a principal place of business outside of North Carolina. This includes international preparation programs.
  (s) “Out-of-state exam” means a standardized examination recognized by the licensing authority of a jurisdiction outside North Carolina that is designed to demonstrate an applicant’s academic and professional preparation in license area and the passage of which is necessary to be issued a renewable license in that jurisdiction.
  (t) “Professional Educator” means an administrator, teacher, or student services personnel.
  (u) “Provisional assistant principal license” means a one-year license, renewable twice, issued to an individual employed as an assistant principal who meets the requirements of LICN-014, Section 7.
  (v) “Renewal cycle” means the five-year period between each renewal of a CPL.
  (w) “Residency license” means a one-year license, renewable twice, issued to an individual who meets the qualifications of LICN-019.
  (x) “State Salary Schedule” means the salary schedule for teachers and other public school personnel administered by the State Board of Education in accordance with G.S. 115C-12(9)a. and 115C-12(16).
  (y) “Student services personnel” means an individual providing specialized assistance to students, teachers, administrators, or the education program in general, as classified by the SBE. Student services personnel shall include individuals employed in school counseling, school social work, school psychology, audiology, speech-language pathology, and media coordination.
  (z) “Student services personnel license” means a 5-year renewable license issued to an individual who meets the requirements of LICN-012.
  (aa) “Teacher” means an individual whose major responsibility is to teach or directly supervise teaching, as classified by the SBE.
3. **General Licensure Requirements**
  (a) Except as otherwise provided by law, any person employed as a professional educator in a North Carolina public school shall hold, or be qualified to hold, a professional educator license issued by the DPI in accordance with SBE policy.
  (b) DPI shall issue the following classifications of professional educator licenses:
    (i) Teacher
    (ii) Administrator
    (iii) Student Services Personnel
  (c) Each license issued by DPI shall include the following information:
    (i) The grade levels, content areas, and specializations for which the applicant shall be eligible for employment.
    (ii) The applicant’s years of professional experience.
    (iii) The applicant’s degree level, which shall be based on the highest degree issued to the applicant by an educator preparation program.
  (d) For any license that requires the applicant to complete an educator preparation program, the EPP must either be recognized by the SBE or by the equivalent licensing authority in the state, country, or other jurisdiction in which the EPP maintains its principal place of business.
  (e) The Department of Public Instruction shall issue a license once the applicant submits a complete license application. An application is considered complete when the applicant has:
    (i) Submitted all information, documentation, and credentials required by LICN-004 (“License Application”), thereby demonstrating qualification for the license;
    (ii) Paid the relevant application fee, as prescribed by LICN-008; and
    (iii) If applicable, been cleared from any investigation conducted by the Superintendent of Public Instruction pursuant to LICN-007.
  (f) A complete application for one of the licensure services specified in LICN-008, except for license renewal, must be submitted to DPI by April 15 of a given fiscal year to guarantee processing of the application prior to the end of the fiscal year. If a complete application is submitted after April 15 and DPI does not approve the application prior to the end of the fiscal year, a public school unit employing the applicant shall not use State or federal funds for the purpose of paying the applicant’s salary for the fiscal year in which the application was submitted.
  (g) Every license shall expire on June 30 of the calendar year in which it is set to expire, unless it is renewed, extended, or converted to another type of license in accordance with SBE policy.
