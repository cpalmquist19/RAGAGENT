## Policy Statement


This Policy establishes the requirements for an administrator to renew a continuing professional license.

## Policy

1. Each person holding an administrator license must renew that license every five years.
2. Administrators must complete eight CEUs during each renewal cycle consisting of the following:
  (a) Four CEUs focused on the administrator’s leadership role in instruction, human resources, and management.
  (b) Four general CEUs that are germane to the K-12 educational environment.
