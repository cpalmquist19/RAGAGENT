## Policy Statement


This Policy establishes the requirements for student services personnel to renew a continuing professional license.

## Policy

1. Each person holding a student services personnel license must renew that license every five years.
2. Student services personnel must complete eight CEUs during each renewal cycle consisting of the following:
  (a) Four CEUs that align with the North Carolina standards for the relevant professional discipline, as outlined in the State Board of Education Policy Series on Qualifications and Evaluation (EVAL).
  (b) Four general CEUs that are germane to the K-12 educational environment.
3. A person serving as a school audiologist or school speech-language pathologist shall be exempt from the continuing education requirements of this Policy so long as the person continues to satisfy the applicable requirements of LICN-012.
